GymFlow

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 September 2026 · Ofir Sofer · hello.gymflow@gmail.com

GymFlow is a workout planning and tracking app for iPhone, published by Ofir Sofer. This policy explains what GymFlow does with your information.

The short version: your training data stays on your iPhone. GymFlow has no accounts, no login, and no servers of its own. The only information that leaves your device is what is needed to process a GymFlow Pro subscription, if you choose to buy one.

Data stored only on your device

All of the following is stored locally on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded to us, and we cannot see it:

  • Workout programs, schedules, and rotations
  • Workout sessions, and the sets, reps, and weights you log
  • Timed and cardio data, including durations and distances
  • Exercises, including any custom exercises you create
  • Progress, history, and calendar data
  • Your profile: display name, weight unit, weekly goal, training goal, experience level, rest timer, appearance, haptics, and language
  • Your profile photo, if you set one
  • Notification and reminder settings

We have no copy of any of it. If you lose or replace your device without transferring your data, we cannot recover it for you.

Subscriptions

GymFlow offers an optional paid subscription, GymFlow Pro. If you subscribe, two third parties are involved:

Apple

Your purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment, billing, renewals, cancellations, and refunds. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to validate purchase receipts and to determine whether your subscription is currently active. Information that may be sent to RevenueCat includes:

  • Purchase and transaction information from your App Store receipt
  • Subscription status, such as whether it is active, when it expires, and whether it will renew
  • The identifier of the product purchased
  • An anonymous app user ID generated by RevenueCat
  • Device and app information collected by the RevenueCat SDK as part of providing the service

GymFlow does not send your workout data, profile data, or profile photo to RevenueCat, to Apple, or to anyone else. RevenueCat receives subscription information only.

GymFlow does not create an account for you and does not attach your name, email address, or any contact information to your subscription. The app user ID is anonymous and generated by RevenueCat.

For details of how RevenueCat handles the information it receives, including retention and international transfers, see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. We do not restate their practices here, because they are theirs to describe and may change.

What GymFlow does not do

  • No user accounts and no login
  • No GymFlow-operated cloud sync or cloud backup
  • No advertising, and no advertising SDKs
  • No ad tracking, and no use of the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA)
  • No tracking of you across other companies' apps or websites
  • No third-party crash reporting
  • No third-party app analytics
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising

Notifications

GymFlow can send workout reminders, rest timer alerts, and timed set alerts. These are local notifications, scheduled by the app on your device. GymFlow does not use push notifications, does not register a push token, and no server sends you anything.

Exporting your data

Profile → Export GymFlow Data creates a JSON file of your local data and hands it to the iOS share sheet, so you can save or send it wherever you choose. The file is created on your device and deleted from the app's temporary storage afterwards. GymFlow does not upload it. Once you share it, where it goes is up to you.

Deleting your data

  • Erase All GymFlow Data (Profile → Erase All GymFlow Data) permanently deletes your local workout history, programs, custom exercises, profile photo, and preferences from your device, and returns the app to a first-use state.
  • Deleting the app removes all of GymFlow's local data from your device.

Neither action cancels a subscription. Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID in the App Store. Neither action deletes records held by Apple or RevenueCat; to ask about information those companies hold, contact us at the address below and we will help where we can.

Age

GymFlow is intended for people aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us.

Fitness and medical disclaimer

GymFlow is a tool for planning and tracking your own training. It is not a substitute for a personal trainer, physician, physiotherapist, or other qualified health or fitness professional, and it does not provide medical diagnosis or medical advice.

GymFlow cannot assess whether any exercise, weight, or training plan is suitable for you. Choosing what to train, how much to lift, and how hard to push is your responsibility, based on your own health, your experience, and any professional guidance you have.

Exercise carries an inherent risk of injury. If you experience pain, dizziness, faintness, unusual shortness of breath, or any other concerning symptoms, stop exercising and seek appropriate medical advice.
