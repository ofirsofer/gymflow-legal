GymFlow

Privacy Policy

Effective 19 September 2026 · Ofir Sofer · hello.gymflow@gmail.com

GymFlow is a workout planning and tracking app for iPhone, published by Ofir Sofer. This policy explains what GymFlow does with your information.

GymFlow's core workout-tracking features — building programs, logging workouts, and tracking progress — do not require an account, and that data is stored primarily on your iPhone. GymFlow also offers optional Coach/Athlete features: connecting with a coach, sharing training data, and receiving assigned workouts. Those features require a free GymFlow account, and if you use them, the specific data needed to support a connected coach is synced to GymFlow's cloud backend. This policy explains exactly what that means below.

Third parties and service providers

GymFlow works with the following companies to provide the app. We do not use any analytics or advertising SDKs, and there are no other third parties involved.

  • Supabase — provides accounts, authentication, and the cloud storage behind Coach/Athlete features. See “Account and sign-in” and “Coach and Athlete features” below, and Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — manages GymFlow Pro and GymFlow Coach subscriptions and determines your entitlement status. See “Subscriptions” below, and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple — processes App Store purchases and subscriptions, and provides Sign in with Apple if you choose to use it. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data stored locally on your device

The following is stored locally on your iPhone, in the app's private storage, and is not part of GymFlow's cloud backend:

  • Workout programs, schedules, and rotations
  • Custom exercises you create
  • Notification and reminder settings
  • App preferences: weight unit, weekly goal, training goal, experience level, rest timer, appearance, haptics, and language
  • A custom profile photo, if you set one — only a small bundled avatar icon, never a photo, is ever used by Coach/Athlete features (see below)

If you don't use Coach/Athlete features, your completed workout sessions, progress, personal records, and body-weight and hydration logs also stay local-only. We have no copy of any of it, and if you lose or replace your device without transferring your data, we cannot recover it for you.

If you use Coach/Athlete features, some of this same training data is synced to GymFlow's cloud backend so a connected coach can see it — see “Coach and Athlete features” below for exactly what, and why.

Account and sign-in

Coach/Athlete features require a free GymFlow account. Creating one is entirely optional — if you only use GymFlow to plan and log your own workouts, you never need one.

GymFlow uses Supabase to provide accounts, authentication, and the cloud storage Coach/Athlete features depend on. When you create a GymFlow account, we store:

  • Your email address, if you sign in with an emailed one-time code
  • Your Apple ID identity, if you sign in with Sign in with Apple — Apple shares only what you approve, typically your name and an email address (or a private relay address) the first time you sign in
  • A unique account identifier generated by Supabase
  • Your display name
  • Whether the account has enabled Coach features
  • A small bundled avatar icon selection, if you choose one for a connected coach to see instead of your initials — never an actual photo

This account data is stored on Supabase's infrastructure and linked to your GymFlow account. It exists to make sign-in and Coach/Athlete features work.

Coach and Athlete features

An athlete connects to a coach by entering an invite code the coach generates and shares with them. Nothing is shared with a coach until you do this — connecting is always an explicit action you take.

Once you're connected, your coach can access:

  • Your workout history and session summaries
  • Exercises, sets, reps, weights, and training volume you've logged
  • Personal records and progress over time, including exercise-level history
  • The status and details of any workout your coach has assigned you

Body weight and hydration logs are different: they are only visible to your coach if you separately turn on sharing for each one, in GymFlow. Both are off by default. Once you're connected to a coach, those entries are synced to GymFlow's backend regardless of the sharing toggle — the toggle controls only whether your coach can see them, so it can take effect immediately without waiting on anything to sync.

A coach can only ever see the athletes who have connected with them this way — never any other GymFlow user, and never by searching or browsing.

Disconnecting a coach (from either side, in the app) removes the coach's access to your protected data immediately, because that access is checked against the active connection every time. Historical records on GymFlow's backend may still exist after disconnecting, and a workout your coach previously assigned to you may remain visible on your own account, since it's treated as your copy of that workout once assigned. Disconnecting a coach is separate from deleting your GymFlow account — see “Deleting your data” below.

Subscriptions

GymFlow offers two optional paid subscriptions, GymFlow Pro and GymFlow Coach. If you subscribe to either, two third parties are involved:

Apple

Your purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment, billing, renewals, cancellations, and refunds. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to validate purchase receipts and to determine whether your subscription is currently active. Information that may be sent to RevenueCat includes:

  • Purchase and transaction information from your App Store receipt
  • Subscription status, such as whether it is active, when it expires, and whether it will renew
  • The identifier of the product purchased
  • An app user ID: anonymous if you have not created a GymFlow account, or your GymFlow account identifier if you have signed in, so a GymFlow Coach subscription can be correctly associated with your account
  • Device and app information collected by the RevenueCat SDK as part of providing the service

GymFlow does not send your workout data, profile data, or profile photo to RevenueCat, to Apple, or to anyone else. RevenueCat receives subscription information, and, if you're signed in, your GymFlow account identifier — nothing more.

If you have not created a GymFlow account, your subscription is not attached to your name, email address, or any contact information — the app user ID is anonymous and generated by RevenueCat. If you have signed in, your subscription is associated with your GymFlow account identifier so your entitlements work correctly across the app.

For details of how RevenueCat handles the information it receives, including retention and international transfers, see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. We do not restate their practices here, because they are theirs to describe and may change.

What GymFlow does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising SDKs
  • No ad tracking, and no use of the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA)
  • No tracking of you across other companies' apps or websites
  • No third-party crash reporting
  • No third-party app analytics
  • No account or cloud sync of any kind, unless you choose to use Coach/Athlete features
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising

Notifications

GymFlow can send workout reminders, rest timer alerts, and timed set alerts. These are local notifications, scheduled by the app on your device. GymFlow does not use push notifications, does not register a push token, and no server sends you anything.

Exporting your data

Profile → Export GymFlow Data creates a JSON file of your local data and hands it to the iOS share sheet, so you can save or send it wherever you choose. The file is created on your device and deleted from the app's temporary storage afterwards. GymFlow does not upload it. Once you share it, where it goes is up to you.

Deleting your data

  • Erase All GymFlow Data (Profile → Erase All GymFlow Data) permanently deletes your local workout history, programs, custom exercises, profile photo, and preferences from this device, and returns the app to a first-use state. This does not delete a GymFlow account, if you have one, or any data already synced to GymFlow's cloud backend.
  • Delete Account (Profile → Account → Delete Account, for signed-in users) permanently deletes your GymFlow account and the Coach/Athlete cloud data associated with it — your account record, coach/athlete connections, and synced training data. It also erases GymFlow's local data on that device, the same as Erase All GymFlow Data above.
  • Deleting the app removes all of GymFlow's local data from your device, but does not delete a GymFlow account, if you have one — use Delete Account first if you want your account and cloud data removed too.

None of these actions cancel a subscription. Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID in the App Store, and are not automatically cancelled by Erase All GymFlow Data or Delete Account.

If you signed in with Apple, you can additionally remove GymFlow's access under your Apple Account's Sign in with Apple settings after deleting your account — on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → GymFlow → Stop Using Apple ID. This is optional, and separate from deleting your GymFlow account.

Neither Erase All GymFlow Data nor Delete Account affects records held by Apple or RevenueCat, who retain information according to their own practices; to ask about information those companies hold, contact us at the address below and we will help where we can.

Data retention

We keep the account and Coach/Athlete data described above for as long as your GymFlow account exists and it's needed to provide those features — there is no separate fixed retention period. Deleting your GymFlow account removes the account and cloud data the Delete Account flow handles, described above. Apple and RevenueCat retain the information they separately hold according to their own practices; see their privacy policies linked above.

Age

GymFlow is intended for people aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us.

Fitness and medical disclaimer

GymFlow cannot assess whether any exercise, weight, or training plan is suitable for you. Choosing what to train, how much to lift, and how hard to push is your responsibility, based on your own health, your experience, and any professional guidance you have.
